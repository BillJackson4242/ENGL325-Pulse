Conversation Shape

Group dynamics snapshot
This measures the shape of group conversation — not individual effort. A group with absent members will show weaker dynamics even when the active members do excellent work. Four dimensions, equally weighted: Reply engagement — are people responding to each other, not just posting in parallel? Thread depth — do conversations develop beyond a single exchange? Participation balance — is the workload shared across the group? Cross-engagement — are members connecting with multiple peers? Labels: Getting Started → Developing → Building → Strong → Exemplary.

Role Fulfillment

Did the roles shape the conversation?
Role fulfillment estimates whether each assigned role shaped the group's analysis. It looks at: Role-relevant language (40%) — did the member use terms, concepts, and analytical moves that align with their assigned role? Engagement with others (25%) — did they build on peers' ideas or stay isolated? Active participation (20%) — did they show up and contribute substantively? Role identification (15%) — did they signal their lens to the group? You don't have to announce "I am the Cast Mapper" to score well — doing the work matters most. Labels: Not Yet → Emerging → Developing → Solid → Strong.
